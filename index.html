OUR STORY

From a Telegram idea to the NEXUS ecosystem

NEXUS started as a simple idea: create an ecosystem where community activity, useful tasks and participation can build measurable value inside one Web3 project.

01

The Beginning

NEXUS began with a Telegram-based community model focused on participation, Points and simple activities that anyone could complete.

02

The Ecosystem

The project evolved into a WebApp with an Airdrop system, tasks, levels, referrals, Tap Game, rewards and a dedicated NXS balance.

03

The Next Stage

The next goal is to connect community activity with NXS distribution, Web3 functionality and future token utility.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What is NEXUS?

NEXUS is a Telegram-based Web3 ecosystem combining games, tasks, Points, referrals, rewards and the future NXS token.

What is NXS?

NXS is the planned utility token of the NEXUS ecosystem. Its future use cases will depend on the development of the ecosystem and announced token utilities.

How do Points become NXS?

The current NEXUS exchange model is 5,000 Points for 1 NXS, subject to the rules implemented in the ecosystem.

Will NXS be tradable?

Trading is a future phase of the roadmap. Any actual DEX or CEX listing will be announced through official NEXUS channels.

Will NXS price increase?

No price increase can be guaranteed. If NXS becomes tradable, its market value will depend on supply, demand, liquidity, market conditions and ecosystem development.

Is NEXUS free to join?

The NEXUS ecosystem is designed to be accessible through Telegram. Some optional premium features may have their own conditions.
